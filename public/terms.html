Kampasika Biz: Rooms, Leases And Fees

  • Browsing rooms and applying for a room through Kampasika is free.
  • When a student signs a lease with a Kampasika Biz landlord through Kampasika, the student pays Kampasika a service fee. The exact amount is shown on the lease before signing. It is a fee for Kampasika's platform services (finding the room, the application, the online lease, records and reminders). It is not rent and not a deposit.
  • The lease becomes active once the service fee is paid. Rent and deposits are paid to the landlord, not to Kampasika.
  • The service fee is not refundable once the lease is active, except where the landlord cancels before move-in, the room was materially misrepresented, or the law requires a refund. Contact support for these cases.
  • Kampasika's fees are collected through licensed payment providers. Kampasika does not hold rent or deposits for landlords.
  • Listing rooms and using Kampasika Biz tools is free for landlords unless Kampasika gives notice of a change in advance.

How Rent Payments Work

  • Kampasika does not receive or hold rent or deposits. Rent is paid directly to the landlord, either to the landlord's own mobile money, Lipa or bank number, or online through a licensed payment provider into the landlord's own account.
  • Kampasika provides software to start, record and track these payments: rent schedules, payment reports, confirmations, reminders and statements.
  • When a student pays the landlord's number directly, the payment is recorded once the landlord confirms receiving it. Students must report payments truthfully, and landlords must confirm or reject them honestly and promptly.
  • Payment disputes about rent are between the student and the landlord. Kampasika may help with records it holds, but it cannot reverse or refund a payment it never received.
  • Landlords are responsible for the accuracy of the payment number they give and for any taxes on rent they receive.
  • Kampasika Biz is open to any landlord. A "Verified" badge means Kampasika checked the landlord's documents; it is not a guarantee. Students should view a room before paying.

No Going Around Kampasika

If a student and a landlord first connect through Kampasika (for example through a room listing, a chat or an application), both agree not to make the tenancy outside Kampasika to avoid Kampasika's fees. This applies for 6 months after they first connect through Kampasika. If this happens, Kampasika may:

  • charge the service fee that would have applied and, where the landlord arranged or encouraged it, charge the landlord an equal amount;
  • suspend or close the accounts involved, remove listings, and remove verified or Kampasika Biz badges;
  • recover unpaid amounts together with reasonable collection costs, including through legal action.

Leases and payments made outside Kampasika do not get Kampasika records, rent reminders, payment history or support in disputes.
